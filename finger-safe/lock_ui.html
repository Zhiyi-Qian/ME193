    What the network sees

    Your hand travels left to right through the three convolution layers. Each small square is one filter’s output; bright means that filter found its pattern there.

    1What it gets

    Your hand squeezed down to a small square of pixels.

    2Layer 1

    Finds simple patterns: edges, and where skin meets the table. Pooling then halves the size.

    3Layer 2

    Combines those edges into curves and finger-shaped pieces. Pooled again.

    4Layer 3

    Reacts to whole fingers and where they sit. After pooling, a linear layer turns it into one score per answer.
